What are the requirements to incorporate a private company in Kenya?
LK
Under the Companies Act 2015, a private limited company requires a minimum of one shareholder and one director. § 11(1)

The company must be registered with the Registrar of Companies and must file a Memorandum and Articles of Association. § 24 The name must not be identical or deceptively similar to an existing registered company. § 56(2)

In Mwangi v Kariuki [2019] eKLR, the Court of Appeal affirmed that failure to file the prescribed forms within 30 days renders the incorporation voidable. CA 2019
